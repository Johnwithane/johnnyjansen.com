# Positioning

> Read before writing any copy. Rewritten on 2026-09-30: johnnyjansen.com is Johnny's general portfolio and resume. It is not the front door of any product business he runs; those have their own sites.

## The one sentence

**A creative director who builds the software too.**

Sub-line: brand, campaigns, video and custom software, by one person.

## Who comes here

- People hiring a creative director: a brand, a launch, a series, a campaign.
- People who need custom software built: a product, a platform, an internal tool.
- People who need both. That is the rare part, and the reason to hire one person.
- Anyone checking Johnny's background. The resume page and PDF serve them.

## The through line (bio)

One sentence carries the whole career: *I have always built systems that turn an audience's ideas into the next thing. First with content, now with software.* Club Penguin (community content loop), LEGO Build Your Own Adventure (voting and UGC as the product), the Ocean Wise series, the music videos as complex planned systems, then Remoose and the products since. Every stop is the same loop with different tools. Write the bio from that line and nothing on it reads as a career change.

## Brand is part of the build

Johnny designed the identity of every product on the site (Remoose, Blue Seal, BetterTour, Pocket Jams, WaldeTree). It is a differentiator against developers (who cannot) and agencies (who charge twice), and it is why the case studies can say the product and the brand read as one.

## The AI question

Disclose first. The site says it is built with AI coding tools and then shows the discipline: default-deny rules with tests, schemas at every boundary, strict types, CI that refuses a red build. The How I build page has to keep earning that claim.

## Project order

1. **Remoose** (flagship). The origin story: the LEGO-era UGC idea, funded in 2022, out of money as AI tools arrived, the two years that made him a builder.
2. **Blue Seal**, with his brother.
3. **BetterTour**, with Drew McTaggart (Dear Rouge) and James Butler.
4. **Pocket Jams**, with his son.
5. **WaldeTree**, for his wife's family.

The film work sits on the About page: the reel, the series, the awards.

## Nothing from an employer

No work product, screenshots, tools, logos, case studies or internal detail from a current or past employer without their written sign-off. A current role appears in the career list as a title and one plain line, nothing more.

## Redaction list

Never on this site, from any of the repos:

- Blue Seal: pricing, margins, Stripe Connect unit costs, the founding cohort scheme, any tradesperson documents.
- BetterTour: competitor screenshots and the audit of them, monetisation mechanics, real tour data.
- Pocket Jams: any user content, usernames, avatars, chat, moderation queues. It is a kids' platform.
- WaldeTree: any real names, photos, dates, tree data.
- Any product business Johnny runs: its clients, prices, prospects and plans.
- Family: names with birth dates, schedules, addresses, anything from the family planner.
- All: keys, secrets, project ids paired with service-account details.

Describe the mechanism, never the data.

## Voice

Short sentences. Plain words. First person. Concrete nouns and real numbers. No em or en dashes. No "seamlessly", "effortlessly", "elevate", "unlock", "robust", "leverage", "not just X but Y", triads. If a sentence would fit on a slide, cut it.
